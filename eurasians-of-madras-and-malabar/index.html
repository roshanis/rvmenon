About this edition

Edgar Thurston, Superintendent of the Madras Government Museum, published this survey of the Eurasian (mixed European and Indian) communities of Madras city and the Malabar coast in the Museum’s anthropology bulletin in the late 1890s (Madras Government Museum Bulletin, Anthropology, Vol. II, pp. 69–130). It describes their family names, occupations, history and physique, and traces the Portuguese and Dutch ancestry of the families of Calicut, Tellicherry, Cannanore and Cochin.

It is a late source, and its race science is of its time, but it records names and family traditions that reach back to the Dutch period on the coast. I read it as background for my fiction set there, and I am sharing it in case it helps anyone else.

This edition follows the original in full, with spelling modernized and short notes. The measurement tables are kept as printed, and the short pieces that follow in the same bulletin are included. Page numbers of the bulletin are marked in the text, so any passage can be checked against the scan.

The edition was made with machine assistance and has not been proofread by a person. Spot-check anything you intend to quote against the original pages.
