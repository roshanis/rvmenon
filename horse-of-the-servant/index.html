The novel

Horse of the Servant

by RV Menon

Blood and Thrones · Book One

Goa, 1738. Nagoji Sawant, a Maratha cavalry officer, is broken in a Portuguese dungeon and shipped south in chains. A monsoon storm tears the slave ship apart. He lives, and washes up on the pepper coast with nothing but what he knows of horses and European war.

That knowledge makes him useful. In Travancore, King Marthanda Varma is crushing the old noble houses and forging a single state, and the Dutch East India Company means to stop him. Nagoji is set to train horsemen for a war the kingdom cannot afford to lose, and in 1741 that war comes to the beach at Colachel.

A stranger who came ashore as wreckage must decide whether he is a guest, a weapon, or a servant.

About the author

RV Menon

RV Menon is a writer who grew up between Kerala and Maharashtra, two lands whose histories intertwine in Horse of the Servant. Its stories draw from memories shared by his father, grandparents, uncles, aunts, mother-in-law, and wife, woven together with historical research into a narrative that honours both family lore and the documented past.

Horse of the Servant is his first novel.
